The Evolution of the Atom

From Dalton to the modern model: five galleries, three experiments, one big idea. Models change when new evidence shows their limits.

Learning goals

Essential question: How did new experimental evidence change scientists’ understanding of atomic structure?

Today I will explore the five historical models of the atom and investigate the evidence that led to each one.

So I can explain how scientists used experimental evidence to revise atomic models over time.

I am successful when I can:
  • Identify and describe all five atomic models and match each scientist to a model.
  • Explain the experiment or evidence behind each model and its major discovery.
  • Explain why earlier models were revised, and compare and contrast models.
  • Arrange the models in chronological order and explain how the modern model differs.
